Put LiveDeck on your TikTok LIVE

  1. Prepare your slides, overlay, board or script here first.
  2. Open TikTok, tap +, swipe to LIVE.
  3. Choose the screen-sharing option (on many accounts it is called Mobile gaming), give the live a title and tap Go LIVE.
  4. Come back to LiveDeck and tap Present. Viewers now see exactly what is on your screen.
  5. To leave Present, tap the faint × at the top of the screen or press Back.

Turn on Do Not Disturb before going live so notifications don't appear on stream. In Present: tap the right or left side to change slides, tap the timer to start or pause it, tap the goal bar to add 1, tap the script to play or pause.

TikTok LIVE needs an 18+ account and usually 1,000 followers. If screen sharing doesn't appear for your account, use TikTok LIVE Studio on a computer instead.
